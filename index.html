How it works

Five stages, each with one job

Water passes through every stage in order. Each one removes something the next stage would struggle with, which is why the membrane lasts years instead of months.

  1. Stage 1

    Sediment

    5 micron polypropylene

    Catches sand, rust and scale flakes from old pipes before they can clog anything downstream.

  2. Stage 2

    Activated carbon

    GAC, coconut shell

    Absorbs chlorine and chloramine, which would otherwise damage the RO membrane.

  3. Stage 3

    Carbon block

    1 micron, NSF 53

    Removes VOCs, pesticides, and the taste and smell people notice most.

  4. Stage 4

    RO membrane

    0.0001 micron, 75 GPD

    Rejects up to 97% of dissolved solids, including lead, arsenic, fluoride, nitrates and PFAS.

  5. Stage 5

    Mineral finish

    Calcium & magnesium

    Adds back a little mineral content and lifts pH to around 7.5, so the water tastes crisp, not flat.

    Questions

    Before you book

    What is TDS, and what's a good number?

    Total dissolved solids is everything dissolved in your water, measured in parts per million (ppm). Most city water reads 150–400 ppm. A well-tuned RO system brings it under 20 ppm. A low number doesn't prove water is safe on its own, which is why our test also checks lead, chlorine, hardness and pH.

    Does reverse osmosis waste a lot of water?

    Our RO-5 uses a permeate pump that brings the ratio to about 1 gallon of drain water for every gallon of drinking water. Older systems ran closer to 4:1.

    Will it remove the minerals I want?

    The membrane removes nearly everything, so stage five adds calcium and magnesium back. Most people say the water tastes better than bottled.

    How long does installation take?

    Under-sink systems take about 2 hours. Whole-home systems usually take half a day and may need a short water shut-off. We clean up and haul away the packaging.

    Is the water test really free?

    Yes. A technician tests at your tap, explains the results, and leaves a written report whether or not you buy anything.
